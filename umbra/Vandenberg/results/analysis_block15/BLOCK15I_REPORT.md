# Block15I report

72 independent synthetic sequences were generated: 24 calibration and 48
held-out evaluation sequences. Under the predeclared persistent-score rule,
0/16 distributed-system A sequences were called B, but 0/32 persistent B
sequences were detected; all 48 evaluations are indeterminate. This is a
negative result for this deliberately simple interpretable rule, not evidence
that the two mechanisms cannot ever be separated.

Seven B sequences have recovered primary-slope error above 10%; none passes
the frozen R²/circular-validity combination. The tested diagnostics therefore
do not demonstrate a hidden large distortion that simultaneously satisfies
those frozen criteria in this compact design.

No real coefficient, frequency, period or depth was altered. The experiment is
intensity-coefficient-only, not a raw SAR simulator; Block15F geometry remains
a separate verified but intentionally unused constant-H control because B terms
are not assigned a wave MTF. **STOP — Block15I complete.**
