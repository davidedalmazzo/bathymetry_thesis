# Block15I frozen protocol

Coefficient-only intensity-Fourier experiment; no real BP12 coefficient enters.
Uses 32 BP12 times, 288×130 grid and exact separable Tukey response. A is a
three-mode finite-band propagating system; B is one propagating mode plus a
nearby static or 5-s OU-slow coefficient. Radial calibration separation is
2.223 bins; held-out evaluation uses 3 radial bins and 1.452 tangential bins.
Ratios .3/.7 are calibration; 1.0 is held-out evaluation. Noise has SNR 10
and iid/5-s variants. Unit is full sequence, not bins/folds.

Block15I rule is frozen before evaluation: absolute amplitude-correlation at
least .85 and M1 gain at least .10 calls persistent; all else indeterminate.
It is deliberately not used on Vandenberg.
