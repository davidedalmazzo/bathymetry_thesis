# Block15G protocol congelato

Synthetic intensity-Fourier coefficient experiment only.  The 32 BP12 mean TxTime and the geometry target/transfer formulation of Block15F are reused; no CPHD signal, stack or real Q2 fit is used. The dominant canonical mode is the frozen `(133,65)`; the observed patch is modes rows 127–141 and cols 64–66. Its spatial templates are the exact DFT of the same separable 2-D Tukey alpha=0.1 window used in Block15B, evaluated at fractional or integer component modes. Noise has spatial covariance from the DFT of window-squared and proper complex OU time covariance at the actual times.

Calibration (seed 150700, 12 replicates/family) contains a constant single component, a geometrically modulated single component, and a geometrically modulated single component plus static offset. Evaluation (seed 150701, 12 per case) contains stress unresolved (`delta mode=.25`, `delta s=.023`), stress temporal (`delta mode=3`, `delta s=.138`), and physical finite-depth h=10 m cases with mode separations 1 or 6 and amplitude ratios .7/.3. Physical slopes use finite-depth dispersion separately for each k; stress slopes are explicitly mathematical, not free waves at one bathymetry.

Q0/Q1/Q2 use global 31×31 variable-projection search on signed [-1,1] rad/s, no truth initialization. Q2 is selected only by purged 8×4 validation gain above `max(.10, 95th calibration gain)`, no boundary, no coarse 1% ambiguity and separation >.02. Primary folds purge two look neighbours; Block15D 4×8 is recorded as secondary control. Recovery uses optimal label exchange and max error <= min(.03, .25 true separation). Monte Carlo realization—not bins/folds/pairs—is the only counting unit.
